Recent work

For the past four years, I have shipped features that help the world's biggest companies go to market — solving for how sellers find content, how teams understand business performance, and how AI surfaces insights that optimize revenue outcomes. The three projects below are highlights from my time at Highspot.

Highspot 0 to 1 Analytics Dashboard

Optimizing business initiatives by transforming fragmented data into insights

Enablement and marketing teams invest in plays, training, and content to run business initiatives, but have no way to track whether reps are following through or whether any of it is driving revenue. I designed the end-to-end product to address this.

Highspot AI-assisted workflow

Improving the content behind AI-generated sales guidance

When sellers ask the Agent for next steps, it pulls guidance from sales playbooks. When that content isn't actionable, the quality of recommendations suffers. I designed an evaluation system that helps authors improve their plays so actions are clear for both sellers and the agent.

Highspot Enterprise search

Making content findable for sellers in large orgs

Poor search relevance was the top reason enterprise customers rated Highspot negatively. I redesigned search to let reps scope results to content that's actually relevant to their role.
