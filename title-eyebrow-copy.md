| Eyebrow (section-label) | Title (section-title / h1) |

| The Problem | Search was driving down NPS and putting customers at risk for churn |
|User needs | Sales reps search with product line, sub-organization, or region in mind. When results are not relevant, they want to filter by those high-level categories. 
| Foundation for a new filtering construct | Content Collections: a new construct for organizing and filtering content |
| Designing the filtering experience | Designing for scoping results before submitting search query and filtering results after submission |
| The Research Finding | Most sales reps will frequently engage with 1–3 collections |
| How this shaped the design | *(no section-title)* |
| redesigning the search dropdown | Modernizing the search dropdown while adding the capability to scope query |
| Handoff | An interactive prototype |
| Results | Search to click conversion is higher when queries are scoped to content collections |
